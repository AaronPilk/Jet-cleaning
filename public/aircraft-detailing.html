Written authorization comes first

Every airport here requires written permission before an outside company does paid work on the ramp. Depending on the field, that means the airport's own permit, an arrangement through an FBO, or working as your contractor under your lease. Before we book, we tell you which one applies to your aircraft at your airport, and we don't turn up until it's settled.

Travel

Travel is included at Albert Whitted, St. Pete–Clearwater, Clearwater Executive, Tampa International, Peter O. Knight and Tampa Executive. Sarasota Bradenton, Venice, Lakeland, Bartow, Brooksville, Plant City and Zephyrhills are a flat $95. Anywhere else in Florida, we quote it.
