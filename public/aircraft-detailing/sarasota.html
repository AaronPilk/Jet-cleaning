How access works at SRQ

SRQ sets the highest bar in the region. The Sarasota Manatee Airport Authority requires an agreement or a permit for any aeronautical activity, including operators who aren't based there, and aircraft detailing is named in its minimum standards. Commercial operators apply for an Annual Operating Permit, and the permit form asks for $5 million in general liability and $5 million in auto coverage. Everyone working airside wears an Authority security badge.

Outside providers are encouraged to work as sublessees of an FBO. Tell us your tail and date and we'll confirm, before anything is booked, how we'd be working at SRQ.

Venice

Venice Municipal is a city airport with a 5,640-foot runway and Skyport Aviation as its FBO. We arrange access through the FBO and work to the City's minimum standards.

Questions we get

What does SRQ require from an outside detailer?

An agreement or permit from the Sarasota Manatee Airport Authority. The Annual Operating Permit form asks for $5 million general liability and $5 million auto, and everyone working airside needs an Authority security badge.

Is there a travel charge to Sarasota?

A flat $95 to SRQ or Venice. Everything else is priced exactly as it is in Tampa and St. Petersburg.
