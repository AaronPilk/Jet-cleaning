Spill & Stain Rescue · short-notice dispatch

Spill and stain rescue, before wheels up

Coffee on cream leather, wine on the carpet, ink on the headliner. Send us a photo and we'll tell you what we can do before we get in the car.

Get an instant estimateCall (704) 877-3511

How it works

  1. A photo first

    What spilled, on what material, and how long ago. It tells us what to bring and whether we can help at all.

  2. Test before we touch

    Every product is tested in a hidden spot. We blot, never rub, and work from the outside of the stain in.

  3. Lift and dry

    Extraction with as little moisture as possible, then fast drying so nothing sets or smells.

  4. Know when to stop

    If dye starts to transfer, the material bleeds, or the stain won't move after two careful attempts, we stop, photograph it and recommend an aircraft upholstery shop. A stain made worse costs more than the job.

Questions we get

How fast can you get there?

Tell us the tail time. Same day is a flat $3,000 response fee on top of the job and under 24 hours is $1,500. Private members have no call-out fee on stain work.

Can every stain come out?

No, and we'll tell you so. If dye transfers, the material bleeds, or the stain won't move after two careful attempts, we stop, photograph it and recommend an aircraft upholstery shop.

What about blood or body fluids?

That's a biohazard. Only someone trained for bloodborne pathogens handles it, with PPE and an EPA-registered disinfectant, following your operator's procedure.
