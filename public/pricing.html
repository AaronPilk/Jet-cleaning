Questions about prices

How much does it cost to detail a private jet in Tampa?

It depends on the size. For a midsize jet such as a Learjet 60 or Citation XLS: a turn clean is $1,250, an exterior dry wash $3,150 and a full interior $3,850. A light jet exterior is $2,450; an ultra-long-range jet such as a G650 is $9,000 for the exterior and $11,000 for the interior.

Why do you publish your prices?

So the price you're quoted is the price you can check. Every job is confirmed in writing from the type and its condition before we arrive, and the number we confirm is the number on the invoice.

Is there a charge for same-day service?

Yes, flat and printed: $3,000 for same day or AOG, $1,500 for under 24 hours, and $750 for a start between 9 p.m. and 5 a.m. or on a holiday. Scheduled work 48 hours or more out has no response fee.

Do you offer discounts?

No. A membership buys a standing slot and a guaranteed response, not a lower price on the work. The only discount we give is fleet terms on a House account for operators with three or more aircraft.

Do you charge travel?

Not at Albert Whitted, St. Pete–Clearwater, Clearwater Executive, Tampa International, Peter O. Knight or Tampa Executive. Sarasota, Venice, Lakeland, Bartow, Brooksville, Plant City and Zephyrhills are a flat $95.
