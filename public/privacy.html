Privacy policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

What we collect

When you request an estimate we collect your name, company, role, email, phone number, aircraft type, airport, requested services, dates and any notes you add.

How we use it

Only to answer your request, schedule and invoice work, and follow up about service. We store it in our customer management system. We never sell or rent your information.

Text messages

We text you only if you check the box asking us to. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and consent are never shared with third parties for marketing.

Aircraft privacy

We don't publish photos, video, tail numbers or customer names without written permission.
