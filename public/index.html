How a job runs

Your procedures first. Paperwork every time.

  1. Book with the aircraft and the time

    Tell us the type, airport, FBO or hangar and when the crew shows. You get a price before we roll.

  2. Walkaround before we touch it

    Photos and a condition record with your crew, and a count of the port covers your maintenance team installed.

  3. Work under your rules

    Operator procedures, FBO ramp rules and airport washing rules come first. Products made for aircraft, used as labeled.

  4. Photo report within two hours

    Before and after from the same angles, what we did, what we used, and anything we noticed and reported to the crew.

What we never do

  • Put water, cleaner or tape on pitot tubes, static ports or angle-of-attack vanes
  • Use hoses or pressure washers on the ramp
  • Use ammonia on windows, windshields or de-ice boots
  • Touch anything that counts as maintenance
  • Enter the flight deck without the crew's OK
  • Post an aircraft, cabin or tail number without written permission

    Questions operators ask

    Before you hand us the keys.

    Are you insured?

    [List your aviation general liability and hangarkeepers limits here once the policy is bound.] We issue certificates to the additional-insured wording your airport, FBO or company requires.

    Do you wash with water?

    Almost never. Our standard exterior is a waterless dry wash with aircraft-spec wash-wax, which fits airport washing rules. Wet washing only happens in an approved wash rack, arranged in advance.

    Do you touch anything maintenance-related?

    No. Your maintenance team installs and removes port covers and does any post-wash checks. We log the covers in and out, stay clear of sensors, and never lubricate anything.

    How fast can you get here?

    Accounts on a program or service agreement get rush response in under 4 hours. For everyone else, call and we'll tell you honestly what we can do. Rush pricing applies under 24 hours.

    Which airports do you work at?

    Tampa Bay airports where we're authorized, or where we work under an FBO or tenant arrangement: [list your airports]. Ask about your field.

    Will you post photos of our aircraft?

    Never without written permission. Tail numbers, cabins and customer names stay private by default.

    What if something gets damaged?

    We photograph the aircraft with your crew before we start, so existing marks are on record. If anything happens on our watch, you hear it from us within 15 minutes.
